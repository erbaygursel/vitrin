Animations

You tested positive. Are you sick?

Bayes' theorem with 10,000 dots: even if a test is 90% accurate, when the disease is rare a positive result means only a 15% chance of being sick. Example numbers, not medical advice.

How: all 10,000 dots are drawn on a single Canvas with seeded random numbers; every figure was checked in Python first. Scenes are timed from the narration and rendered frame by frame with HyperFrames.

YKDS Workbook — trailer

Seven sections in 50 seconds for an exam-prep workbook. A cucumber mascot, curtain / iris / zoom transitions, narration and subtitles.

How: transitions are computed from time; overlapping GSAP animations were hiding the curtain, and that is how it was fixed. Narration with edge-tts, silences trimmed with ffmpeg.

Binary search

Finding a number in a sorted list by discarding half of it at every step.

Why: the algorithm's one requirement is "the list must be sorted"; the discarded half is drawn as a dashed box, and the closing stamp stresses that requirement.

Memory hierarchy in 3D

From registers to disk: the speed, size and technology of every layer in one pyramid.

How: a three.js scene rendered frame by frame with HyperFrames; each layer's duration is computed from the narration.
